About Us

20+ Years of Southwest Florida Interior Design & Renovation

Interior Motives has spent more than 20 years helping Southwest Florida homeowners turn houses into homes they love — from a single room refresh to a full custom build.

Design and Renovation, Start to Finish

We're a licensed interior design and renovation company serving Sarasota, Venice, Port Charlotte, and the surrounding Gulf Coast. Unlike a design-only studio, we carry projects all the way through — from the first layout sketch to the last coat of paint — and unlike a general contractor, design is baked into everything we build, not bolted on afterward.

Over 500 completed projects later, that combination is still what sets us apart: one team accountable for how a space looks and how it's built.

For ground-up custom homes, we bring in Brunderman Building as our construction partner, so your project gets dedicated design attention and dedicated building expertise, working from the same plan.
