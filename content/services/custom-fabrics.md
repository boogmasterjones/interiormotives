        <h2>Fabric in the Florida Sun</h2>
        <p>Strong light fades some fibers faster than others, and humidity is hard on natural weaves. We steer sun-facing rooms toward fade-resistant and solution-dyed fabrics, and save the delicate goods for rooms that stay shaded.</p>
