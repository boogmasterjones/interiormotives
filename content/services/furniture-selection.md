        <h2>Furniture That Lives Outside</h2>
        <p>A Florida lanai is a room, and it deserves the same planning as the living room — but not the same materials. Powder-coated aluminum, teak, and all-weather woven frames hold up where other pieces rust or split, and solution-dyed cushions keep their color through a long season of sun.</p>
        <h2>Sourcing Through the Trade</h2>
        <p>Much of the best-made furniture is only available through designers. We visit showrooms and markets regularly, compare quality and lead times, and bring you a short list of options that fit your budget — so you're choosing between good answers, not wading through hundreds of catalogs.</p>
