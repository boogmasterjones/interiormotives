        <h2>Scale Matters</h2>
        <p>A fixture that looks dramatic online can disappear under a high Florida ceiling, and one that's too large overwhelms a breakfast nook. We size fixtures to ceiling height, table length, and sight lines so each one reads the way it should.</p>
