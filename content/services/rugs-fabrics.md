        <h2>Rugs for Coastal Living</h2>
        <p>Sand, wet feet, and salt air are hard on floor coverings. Flat weaves and performance fibers clean up easily and dry fast, while looser, high-pile constructions are better saved for bedrooms and other low-traffic rooms.</p>
