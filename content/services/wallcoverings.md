        <h2>Wallcoverings in Florida Homes</h2>
        <p>Coastal humidity and strong afternoon light change how some materials perform. Natural grasscloth brings beautiful texture but can fade in direct sun and isn't a fit for wet rooms; scrubbable vinyl and commercial-grade wallcoverings handle bathrooms and high-traffic halls. We match the material to the room, not just the look.</p>
