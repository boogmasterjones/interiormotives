        <p>Southwest Florida homes are built around glass — sliders to the lanai, clerestory windows, and two-story great rooms.         <h2>Function First, Then Beautiful</h2>
        <p>We start by asking how each room is used — a bedroom that needs full blackout, a great room that needs daytime glare control, a street-facing office that needs privacy — and then design treatments that solve that problem and still look like they belong in the room.</p>
