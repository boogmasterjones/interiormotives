Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens at the initial consultation?

We come to your home, look at the rooms you have in mind, and listen — how you live in the space, what bothers you about it, and what you're hoping it becomes. The initial consultation is complimentary, a $250 value.

Do you only design, or do you handle the renovation too?

Both. We design the space and can carry the project through construction and installation, so the people who planned it are the same people making sure it's built right.

Where do you work?

We're based in Charlotte Harbor and regularly work throughout Charlotte, Sarasota, and Lee Counties — including Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte, Boca Grande, Englewood, North Port, Venice, Nokomis, Sarasota, and Cape Coral. If your town isn't listed, give us a call.

Can you work with my builder on a new home?

Yes. For new construction we work alongside your builder on layouts, finishes, and furnishings so the design decisions are made early — before they become change orders.
