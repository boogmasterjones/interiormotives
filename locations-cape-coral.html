Interior Designer in Cape Coral, FL

Cape Coral has more canals than any other city in the world, and most homes here are built around that water — docks, lanais, and pool decks are as central to the design as any interior room.

Designing for Cape Coral Homes

With canal-front lots throughout the city, Cape Coral homes are often designed to blur the line between indoor living space and the water outside — sightlines to the canal, indoor-outdoor flow, and finishes that hold up to a waterfront climate all factor into the design.

Whether you're updating a canal-front home or planning a larger renovation, we bring 20+ years of experience and 500+ completed projects to every Cape Coral job.
