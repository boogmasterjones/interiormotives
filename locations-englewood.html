Designing for Englewood Homes

Englewood straddles the Sarasota–Charlotte county line, and its homes reflect that easygoing spot between Venice and Port Charlotte: Old Florida cottages near the historic Dearborn Street district, canal and bayfront homes along Lemon Bay, and beach condos and vacation homes out on Manasota Key. Many are second homes or long-held family houses that are ready for a refresh.

Salt air, sand, and strong Gulf light shape the decisions here — performance fabrics, finishes that hold up to humidity, and window treatments that tame the glare without hiding the water. Englewood is a short drive from our Charlotte Harbor studio, so in-home consultations are easy to schedule.
