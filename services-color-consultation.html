Color Consultation

A whole-home color palette that flows from room to room, ties your finishes together, and actually holds up to Florida light.

Call (941) 456-4136 Request a Consultation

What's Included

Color is one of the fastest ways to change how a home feels — and one of the easiest to get wrong without a plan. Depending on scope, that typically includes:

  • In-home walkthrough to see your rooms in your actual lighting
  • A whole-home palette that flows logically from room to room
  • Coordinated wall, trim, cabinet, and accent color selections
  • Finish and sheen recommendations suited to Florida humidity and light
  • Coordination with our renovation team if painting or refinishing is part of the plan

How It Works

  1. Consultation

    We walk your home together and talk through the mood you're after.

  2. Design Plan

    We put together a full palette and finish selections for your review.

  3. Refine

    We adjust the palette with your feedback until it's exactly right.

  4. Bring It to Life

    We source, coordinate, and — if needed — paint or refinish to make it real.
