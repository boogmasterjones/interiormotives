Custom Fabrics

Drapery fabric, pillows, cushions, and table linens — chosen from to-the-trade lines and cut, sewn, and finished to fit your rooms.

Request a Consultation Call (941) 456-4136

Fabric Is Where a Room Gets Its Personality

Paint and furniture set the frame; fabric is what people actually touch. A single bolt chosen well — the right weave, the right weight, the right scale of pattern — ties a room together in a way an off-the-shelf pillow never quite does.

What We Help With

  • Fabric selection from to-the-trade lines you won't find in retail stores
  • Drapery, pillow, cushion, and table linen fabrics coordinated across a room
  • Performance and indoor/outdoor weaves for families, pets, and lanais
  • Trim, banding, tape, and edge details that make a piece look custom
  • Yardage calculations, memo samples, and ordering
  • Coordination with the workroom for cutting, sewing, and finishing

Fabric in the Florida Sun

Strong light fades some fibers faster than others, and humidity is hard on natural weaves. We steer sun-facing rooms toward fade-resistant and solution-dyed fabrics, and save the delicate goods for rooms that stay shaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you match fabric to something I already own?

Usually. Bring us the piece or a cutting and we'll pull coordinating options in the right tone and scale.

What does “to the trade” mean?

Some fabric lines are only sold through designers. We have access to those lines and order on your behalf.

Part of a Bigger Project?

Custom Fabrics is usually one layer of a full room or whole-home design. The initial consultation is complimentary.
