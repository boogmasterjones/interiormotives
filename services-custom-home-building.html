Custom Home Building

Ground-up custom home design, delivered in partnership with Brunderman Building — one team accountable for how your new home looks and how it's built.

Call (941) 456-4136 Or Plan a Future Call

Built in Partnership with Brunderman Building

Custom home construction is delivered by our building partner, Brunderman Building, working from the same design plan our team creates with you — so your design vision and your construction team are aligned from day one, not handed off between strangers.

What's Included

Building a custom home means dozens of design decisions long before the first wall goes up. Depending on scope, that typically includes:

  • Whole-home floor plan and space planning tailored to your lot and lifestyle
  • Interior design across every room, from kitchen to primary suite
  • Whole-home color, material, and finish palette
  • Construction delivered by Brunderman Building, from foundation to final walkthrough

How It Works

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your lot, goals, and budget for the new home.

  2. Design Plan

    We design the full home — layout, rooms, and finishes — for your review.

  3. Refine

    We adjust the plan with your feedback until it's exactly right.

  4. Build

    Brunderman Building constructs your home from our shared plan.
