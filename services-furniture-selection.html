The Right Scale, the Right Comfort

The most common furniture mistake isn't style — it's scale. A sofa that looked right in the showroom swallows the room at home, or a dining table leaves no space to walk around it. We plan every piece against real measurements and traffic flow before anything is ordered.

What We Help With

  • Furniture layouts drawn to scale for each room
  • Sofa, sectional, and seating selection for comfort, depth, and durability
  • Dining, bedroom, and occasional furniture chosen to work together
  • Outdoor and lanai furniture built for sun, salt air, and afternoon rain
  • Performance fabrics and finishes for families, pets, and coastal living
  • Ordering, tracking, receiving, delivery, and placement on install day

Furniture That Lives Outside

A Florida lanai is a room, and it deserves the same planning as the living room — but not the same materials. Powder-coated aluminum, teak, and all-weather woven frames hold up where other pieces rust or split, and solution-dyed cushions keep their color through a long season of sun.

Sourcing Through the Trade

Much of the best-made furniture is only available through designers. We visit showrooms and markets regularly, compare quality and lead times, and bring you a short list of options that fit your budget — so you're choosing between good answers, not wading through hundreds of catalogs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work with furniture I already own?

Absolutely. We often keep favorite pieces and design around them, recommending new pieces only where they'll make a real difference.

Do you handle delivery?

Yes. We coordinate ordering, receiving, and delivery, and place everything in the room so it's ready to live in.

Is outdoor furniture really different?

Yes — frames, hardware, and fabrics all have to tolerate salt air and moisture. Indoor pieces moved to a lanai tend to fail quickly here.

Part of a Bigger Project?

Indoor & Outdoor Furniture is usually one layer of a full room or whole-home design. The initial consultation is complimentary.
