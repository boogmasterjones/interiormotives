Space Planning

Smart, livable layouts that make the most of your square footage — whether that's an open-concept great room or a tricky, oddly-shaped floor plan.

Call (941) 456-4136 Or Plan a Future Call

What's Included

Good space planning is felt more than seen — traffic flows naturally, furniture fits without crowding, and every room has a clear purpose. Depending on scope, that typically includes:

  • On-site measurements and an as-built floor plan of your space
  • Furniture and traffic-flow layout options for each room in scope
  • Recommendations for furniture scale, sightlines, and focal points
  • Guidance on structural changes if a layout calls for moving a wall or opening a space
  • Coordination with our renovation team if the layout requires construction work

How It Works

  1. Consultation

    We walk your space together and talk through how you actually use each room.

  2. Design Plan

    We put together layout options for your review.

  3. Refine

    We adjust the plan with your feedback until it's exactly right.

  4. Bring It to Life

    We source, coordinate, and — if needed — renovate to make it real.
