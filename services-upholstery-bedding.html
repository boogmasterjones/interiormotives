Upholstery & Bedding

New upholstery for the pieces worth keeping, custom headboards and benches, and layered bedding that finally pulls a bedroom together.

Request a Consultation Call (941) 456-4136

Keep the Frame, Change the Room

A well-made sofa or a set of dining chairs is often worth far more than what replaces it. If the frame and joinery are solid, new fabric and fresh cushions can make a piece look better than the day it arrived — and keep something you already like.

What We Help With

  • Reupholstery for sofas, chairs, ottomans, and dining seats
  • Custom headboards, benches, and banquettes built to your dimensions
  • Cushion refills, slipcovers, and repairs to worn seating
  • Custom bedding — duvets, coverlets, shams, bed skirts, and accent pillows
  • Performance fabrics for households with kids, pets, or a busy guest season
  • Coordination with the upholstery workroom, pickup, and delivery

Bedding That Suits the Climate

Florida bedrooms rarely need the heavy layers a northern house does. We build beds that look full and finished with breathable layers you can actually sleep under, and choose washable goods for guest rooms that turn over often.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is reupholstering worth it, or should I just buy new?

It depends on the frame. If the piece is solidly built and the proportions work for the room, reupholstery usually buys more quality for the money than a comparable new piece.

Do you make custom headboards?

Yes. Headboards are built to your bed size, wall height, and fabric choice, so the scale is right for the room.

Part of a Bigger Project?

Upholstery & Bedding is usually one layer of a full room or whole-home design. The initial consultation is complimentary.
