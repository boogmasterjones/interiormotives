Why Wallcoverings Are Back

Paint covers a wall; a wallcovering gives it texture, depth, and a point of view. A single grasscloth wall behind a bed or a patterned powder room can do more for a space than any piece of furniture — and today's materials are far more durable and easier to maintain than the papers many people remember.

What We Help With

  • Choosing between grasscloth, textured vinyl, silk, metallic, and printed papers for each room
  • Scale and pattern decisions — what reads as calm versus busy in your specific space
  • Material choices suited to Southwest Florida humidity, bathrooms, and sun exposure
  • Accent walls, ceilings, niches, powder rooms, and full-room applications
  • Sampling in the studio so you see the real texture before anything is ordered
  • Quantities, ordering, and coordination with an experienced installer

Wallcoverings in Florida Homes

Coastal humidity and strong afternoon light change how some materials perform. Natural grasscloth brings beautiful texture but can fade in direct sun and isn't a fit for wet rooms; scrubbable vinyl and commercial-grade wallcoverings handle bathrooms and high-traffic halls. We match the material to the room, not just the look.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can wallpaper go in a bathroom in Florida?

Yes, with the right material. We typically steer bathrooms toward vinyl or commercial-grade wallcoverings that tolerate moisture, and pair them with good ventilation.

Do you install the wallpaper yourselves?

We handle selection, ordering, and scheduling, and coordinate installation with experienced wallcovering installers so the seams and pattern match are done right.

Part of a Bigger Project?

Wallpaper & Wallcoverings is usually one layer of a full room or whole-home design. The initial consultation is complimentary.
