Our People

Meet the Interior Motives Design Team

A small, owner-led studio in Charlotte Harbor: Holly and Lori, two designers who've each run their own firms, and Margaret, a design coordinator who came to interiors by way of residential drafting.

Designer & Owner

Holly

Holly received her design degree at IADT in Tampa, Florida, and went on to own A Point of You Interiors in St. Pete for 15 years.

Today she co-owns Interior Motives Design with Lori, working with clients by appointment across Southwest Florida.

Designer & Owner

Lori

Lori was the owner of a landscape design company for 25 years, then spent 11 years as Design Coordinator at Home Showcase Interiors.

Today she co-owns Interior Motives Design with Holly, working with clients by appointment across Southwest Florida.

Call Lori · (941) 456-4136

How the Studio Began

Two Firms’ Worth of Experience, One Studio

Holly and Lori met in 2023 in Punta Gorda. They soon became partners, combining their experience to open the Interior Motives Design Studio in Charlotte Harbor.

Their passion for interior design continues in creating beautiful spaces throughout Florida.

Designer · Design Coordinator

Margaret

Margaret joined the team as Design Coordinator, bringing a strong passion for design and a unique background in residential drafting. She worked for two engineering firms, gaining hands-on experience drafting residential homes, aluminum structures, and septic design.

Wanting to take that experience a step further, she completed her Interior Design Studies through Florida Gulf Coast University and earned her certification in Residential Interior Design in November 2024.

How We Work

What It's Like to Work With Us

  1. We Listen First

    The initial consultation is complimentary and it's mostly us asking questions about how you live.

  2. You See It Before You Buy It

    Layouts, palettes, and selections are presented for your review before anything is ordered.

  3. Same Team, Start to Finish

    The people who design your space are the same people coordinating the install.

  4. By Appointment

    We meet you at your home or at the studio in Charlotte Harbor — whichever suits the project.
